# Evaluation of Magist as a Marketplace Partner for ENIAC

## Project Overview

ENIAC is a company that sells premium Apple-compatible accessories and is considering expanding through the Brazilian e-commerce platform Magist.

The purpose of this project is to evaluate whether Magist would be a suitable marketplace partner by analyzing product sales, seller activity, delivery performance, and regional demand patterns using SQL.

The analysis is based on the Magist e-commerce database and aims to support a business decision with data-driven insights.

---

## Business Questions

### Product Analysis

* What technology product categories are available on Magist?
* How many technology products have been sold?
* What percentage of total sales comes from technology products?
* What is the average product price?
* Are expensive technology products popular?

### Seller Analysis

* How many months of data are available?
* How many sellers operate on Magist?
* How many sellers offer technology products?
* What percentage of sellers are technology sellers?
* How much revenue is generated by all sellers compared to technology sellers?
* What is the average monthly revenue generated?

### Delivery Analysis

* What is the average time between purchase and delivery?
* How many orders are delivered on time versus delayed?
* Are technology products more likely to experience delivery delays?

### Geographic Analysis

* Which Brazilian states generate the most orders?
* Which states purchase the most technology products?

## Methodology

1. Classified products into **Tech** and **Non-Tech** categories.
2. Measured sales volume and market share of technology products.
3. Identified and analyzed technology-focused sellers.
4. Compared revenue generated by all sellers versus technology sellers.
5. Evaluated delivery performance and delay patterns.
6. Analyzed customer demand across Brazilian states.
7. Developed a business recommendation based on the findings.

---

## Key Findings

* Technology products represent a relatively small share of total marketplace sales.
* Technology-focused sellers account for only a small portion of all sellers on Magist.
* Technology products generate revenue but do not dominate the platform.
* Delivery performance for technology products is generally reliable.
* Customer demand is concentrated in a limited number of Brazilian states.

---

## Business Recommendation

Based on the analysis, Magist does not appear to be a technology-focused marketplace.

While delivery performance is acceptable and there is measurable demand for technology products, the limited presence of technology sellers and the relatively small share of technology sales suggest that Magist may not be the strongest long-term platform for ENIAC's premium Apple-compatible accessories.

A small-scale pilot launch could still be valuable for testing customer demand before making a larger commitment.

**Final Recommendation:** Use Magist as a testing channel rather than a primary growth platform.
