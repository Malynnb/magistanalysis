# Evaluation of Magist for ENIAC Partnership



About the project
This project analyzes whether Magist would be a good business partner for ENIAC, a company that sells 100% Apple‑compatible tech accessories.
Using the Magist e‑commerce dataset, we examine:
	•	Product mix and market fit for tech products
	•	Seller landscape and revenue share for tech vs non‑tech
	•	Delivery performance and delay patterns
	•	Geographic demand within Brazil
	•	Product reviews and ratings

The goal is to provide a data‑driven recommendation on whether ENIAC should move forward with a partnership with Magist.

# Business questions we answer

1 – In relation to the products
	•	What categories of tech products does Magist have?
	•	How many products of these tech categories have been sold?
	•	What percentage of total products sold do tech categories represent?
	•	What’s the average price of products sold?
	•	Are expensive tech products popular?
2 – In relation to the sellers
	•	How many months of data are included in the Magist database?
	•	How many sellers are there?
	•	How many tech sellers are there? What percentage of total sellers are tech sellers?
	•	What is the total amount earned by all sellers and by tech sellers?
	•	What is the average monthly income for all sellers and for tech sellers?
3 – In relation to the delivery time
	•	What’s the average time between order placement and delivery?
	•	How many orders are delivered on time vs delayed?
	•	Is there any pattern for delayed orders (e.g., big products vs tech, high‑value vs low‑value)?
4 – Extra insights (geography & reviews)
	•	Which Brazilian states generate the most orders?
	•	Which Brazilian states buy the most tech products?
