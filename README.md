# Evaluation of Magist as a Marketplace Partner for ENIAC

## Project Overview

ENIAC is a technology company specializing in premium Apple-compatible accessories. Before expanding into a new marketplace, ENIAC needed to determine whether Magist's e-commerce platform could support sustainable growth for high-end technology products.

This project uses SQL-based exploratory and business analysis on the Magist marketplace dataset to evaluate:

* Product-market fit for technology products
* Revenue potential for tech sellers
* Marketplace seller ecosystem
* Delivery performance and logistics
* Customer demand across Brazil
* Customer satisfaction through reviews

The final objective is to provide a data-driven recommendation on whether ENIAC should partner with Magist.

---

## Business Questions

### Product Analysis

* What technology product categories exist on Magist?
* How many tech products have been sold?
* What percentage of total sales are tech-related?
* What is the average product price?
* Are expensive technology products popular?

### Seller Analysis

* How many sellers operate on Magist?
* How many sellers specialize in technology products?
* What percentage of the marketplace is tech-focused?
* How much revenue is generated by tech sellers?
* What is the average monthly income of sellers?

### Delivery & Logistics Analysis

* What is the average delivery time?
* How frequently are orders delayed?
* Are technology products affected differently than other product categories?

### Geographic Analysis

* Which Brazilian states generate the highest number of orders?
* Which states purchase the most technology products?

---

## Key Insights

### Technology Products Represent a Small Segment

Tech-related products account for only a limited share of overall marketplace activity, suggesting that Magist is not primarily focused on technology retail.

### Limited Tech Seller Ecosystem

Only a small proportion of sellers specialize in technology products, reducing opportunities for a strong tech-focused marketplace presence.

### Reliable Delivery Performance

Technology products generally experienced acceptable delivery times and fewer logistics challenges than larger product categories.

### Strong Regional Demand Concentration

Customer demand is concentrated in a handful of Brazilian states, creating opportunities for targeted expansion strategies.

---

## Business Recommendation

Based on the analysis, Magist does not appear to be an ideal long-term marketplace for ENIAC's premium Apple-compatible accessories.

Key reasons:

* Limited technology market share
* Relatively small technology seller ecosystem
* Lower visibility for premium tech products

However, a controlled pilot launch could still provide valuable insights into customer demand and purchasing behavior before committing to a larger partnership.

**Final Recommendation:** Proceed with caution and use Magist as a testing channel rather than a primary sales platform.
